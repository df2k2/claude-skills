# Support

How you get help depends on the product and your license. Our commercial products come with a support subscription and a technical support form. The open source Hyvä Theme is supported by the community on Slack.

We try to handle support as transparent as possible, so that anyone can learn from it. That's why we prefer to answer questions on [Slack](#community-support-on-slack).

## Where to Ask

| Your question | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Technical, about a commercial product | [Technical support form](https://www.hyva.io/request-hyva-support/request/index/) in your customer dashboard |
| Technical, about the open source Hyvä Theme | [Hyvä Slack community](https://www.hyva.io/slack) |
| Sales, licenses, invoices | [Contact form](https://www.hyva.io/contact) or email |
| A security vulnerability | [Reporting a Vulnerability](../hyva-themes/faqs/security-compliance.html#reporting-a-vulnerability) |

GitLab and GitHub are not support channels

Our repositories are for source code and contributions, see [Source Code and Contributing](source-code.html). Report issues through the technical support form or Slack, so they can be confirmed by the Hyvä team.

## Technical Support for Commercial Licenses

Customers with a license for a commercial Hyvä product can submit technical support questions through the [technical support form](https://www.hyva.io/request-hyva-support/request/index/) in their account dashboard. Technical support questions sent by email receive a standard reply pointing back to that form and the Slack community.

Hyvä Commerce, Hyvä Checkout, and Hyvä Enterprise come with a support subscription. Everyone in Slack who works on a project for a license holder with an active subscription can ask questions in that product's Slack channel as well. For the best response times, use the technical support form.

## Community Support on Slack

We use [Slack](https://www.hyva.io/slack) as a community support channel for all things Hyvä. The following is an overview of the main channels and what they are used for. Before posting, please read the [community guidelines](index.html#slack) in our Onboarding Guide.

Sensitive Data

Remember that other people can see support requests on Slack, so be sure not to post sensitive data in support requests!
Remove or hide customer information, passwords, credit card numbers, credentials, etc. before posting.

### Hyvä Theme Channels

The open source Hyvä Theme comes with community Slack support. The Hyvä Team reads every message and tries to be as helpful as possible, but we have to rely on our community to help each other due to the limited time of a small team.

`#general`
:   General questions and discussions that don't fit anywhere else.

`#hyva-themes`
:   Any question specific to the Hyvä Theme can go here.

`#tailwind-talk`
:   Anything TailwindCSS related.

`#alpine-talk`
:   Discussions and questions about Alpine.js.

`#a11y`
:   Accessibility related discussions and questions.

`#performance`
:   Like talking about tuning frontend performance? This is the right channel for you.

`#extensions`
:   If you are looking for a Hyvä compatible extension, or have questions about compatibility modules, this is the channel for you.

`#magewire`
:   Questions about Magewire, unless they are Hyvä Checkout related, in which case use `#hyva-checkout`.

`#pagebuilder`
:   To discuss how to use PageBuilder with Hyvä, please use this channel.

`#magento-commerce`
:   This channel is for talking about Adobe Commerce and B2B in relation to Hyvä.

`#luma-checkout`
:   Questions and discussions about Luma Checkout and other theme fallback checkouts.

`#job-listings`
:   If you are looking for paid help, this is where you can post job details.

`#update-notifications`
:   We announce all product releases here.

### Hyvä Commerce Channels

`#hyva-cms`

### Hyvä Checkout Channels

`#hyva-checkout`

## Sales and Licensing

Any sales or license related questions can be asked via email or our [contact form](https://www.hyva.io/contact). Your licenses, orders, and invoices are managed in the [Hyvä Portal](the-hyva-portal/index.html).
